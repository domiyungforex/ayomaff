# Project Overview — Ayo Maff Cinematic Artist Website

## 1. Executive Summary

This project delivers an interactive, cinematic, production-ready website concept for Nigerian Afrobeats and Street-Pop star **Ayo Maff** ("Prince of the Street"). Conceived as a digital music experience created by a premier creative studio, the platform brings together an interactive music visualizer, dynamic discography, official music video theater, verified concert listings, merchandise shop, fan community, and an administrative CMS.

## 2. The Artist: Ayo Maff

- **Name**: Ayorinde Mafoluku Ayodele
- **Origin**: Bariga, Lagos State, Nigeria
- **Musical Identity**: Street-Pop, Afrobeats, African Rap, Afro-Adura
- **Breakthrough**: *"Dealer"* featuring Fireboy DML (2024), topping Nigerian charts and earning millions of streams globally.
- **Key Releases**: 
  - *Maffian* EP (2024)
  - *Prince of the Street* (2025/2026)
  - Singles: *"Dealer"*, *"Are You There?"*, *"Last Week"*, *"7 Days"*, *"Malaria"*, *"Lifestyle (YA MAN)"*

## 3. Product Vision & Tone

- **Aesthetic**: Moody, high-contrast, atmospheric street luxury. Deep emerald blacks (`#050806`, `#07140C`) illuminated by high-voltage neon electric green (`#62FF70`, `#B2FF59`).
- **Sound-Reactive**: Every page feels sonically alive. Audio playback drives real-time Canvas waveforms, 3D rotating vinyl, and subtle reactive typography.
- **Editorial Typography**: Brutalist headline tracking paired with sleek, readable body copy and authentic Nigerian street storytelling.

## 4. Key Modules

1. **Global Persistent Music Player & Visualizer**: Continuous playback across page routes, real-time Web Audio API frequency analysis, scrubbing, volume control, and playlist queuing.
2. **Discography**: Filterable releases with full tracklists, lyrics, streaming links (Spotify, Apple Music, Audiomack, YouTube), and credits.
3. **Official Videos**: High-definition video gallery with responsive YouTube embeds.
4. **Tour Schedule**: Live concert calendar with calendar sync (`.ics`) and verified ticketing links.
5. **Visual Archive**: Masonry photo gallery with full-screen lightbox and keyboard navigation.
6. **About Page**: Deep editorial narrative covering Bariga origins, early struggle, rise through "Dealer", musical philosophy, and press quotes.
7. **Merchandise Store**: Product listings, size/color variant selection, shopping cart drawer, and Paystack-ready checkout architecture.
8. **Fan Club**: VIP newsletter signup with custom preferences and fan perks.
9. **Electronic Press Kit (EPK)**: Dedicated media hub for journalists, promoters, and festivals.
10. **Bookings & Inquiries**: Validated forms with spam protection for booking requests and general messages.
11. **Admin CMS**: Dedicated portal for managing releases, videos, events, photos, merch, inquiries, and subscriber lists.
