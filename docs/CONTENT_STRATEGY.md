# Content Strategy & Editorial Integrity

## 1. Editorial Voice & Narrative

Ayo Maff's brand is rooted in authentic Nigerian street culture, hailing from Bariga, Lagos. The tone of the website balances raw street-hop energy ("Prince of the Street", Afro-Adura prayers, hustler resilience) with international artistic sophistication.

### Key Content Pillars
1. **The Bariga Roots**: Growing up in Somolu/Bariga, navigating hardship, leaving home early to support family, turning pain into songs.
2. **The Breakthrough ("Dealer")**: The viral 2024 collaboration with Fireboy DML that cemented Ayo Maff as an essential voice of modern Afrobeats.
3. **The Street Philosophy (Afro-Adura & Rap)**: Deep, resonant baritone delivery, lyrics speaking to youth ambition, prayers for prosperity, and street accountability.
4. **Verified Discography**: Focus on genuine projects (*Maffian* EP, *Prince of the Street*, *"Are You There?"*, *"7 Days"*, *"Last Week"*, *"Dealer"*).

---

## 2. Fact Verification & Disclosure Framework

To ensure 100% journalistic and brand integrity, content is classified into:
- **Verified Facts**: Confirmed through official DSPs (Spotify, Apple Music, Audiomack), YouTube artist channel, and established music journalism (Guardian Nigeria, Culture Custodian, AllMusic).
- **Concept Content**: Unofficial demo elements such as conceptual merchandise, proposed tour dates, and concept booking interfaces, which are marked with clear concept disclosures.
- **Strictly Prohibited**: No fabricated streaming numbers, fake Grammy/Headies nominations, fabricated artist quotes, or deceptive official management statements.
